# Pawdub website

Website, privacy policy, terms of service and data deletion page for **Pawdub: Funny Pet Translator**, an Android app by Hartmann Studios. Your pet finally has subtitles.

- Home: https://chartmann1590.github.io/pawdub-site/
- Get it on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.charles.pawdub
- Privacy Policy: https://chartmann1590.github.io/pawdub-site/privacy/
- Terms of Service: https://chartmann1590.github.io/pawdub-site/terms/
- Delete your data: https://chartmann1590.github.io/pawdub-site/delete/

Support: [open an issue](https://github.com/chartmann1590/pawdub-site/issues/new). Issues are public, so please don't post videos of your home or personal information.

1. The app

Pawdub uses your phone's camera and microphone, or a video you choose, to detect your pet's sounds and the scene on your device, and shows funny subtitle lines written for the persona you pick. You can translate subtitles, have them read aloud, export videos with the subtitles burned in, and make stickers and posters.

Pawdub is for entertainment. The subtitles are jokes written in advance and chosen by the app; they are not a translation of what your pet means, and the app does not understand animals.

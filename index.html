Features

Smart where it counts. All on your phone.

The AI in Pawdub runs on the device: it hears the sound and sees the scene. No cloud, no account, and it keeps working in airplane mode once your languages are downloaded.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Pawdub really translate what my pet is saying?

No, and no app can. Pawdub really detects your pet's sounds and the scene, right on your phone. The words are jokes we wrote for each persona, not what your pet means. It's for fun.

Then what does the AI actually do?

Two kinds of on-device models. One listens and recognizes the sound: bark, whine, growl, howl, meow, purr or hiss. The other looks at the frame: is there a dog or cat, a door, a food bowl, a couch, a toy. Pawdub uses both to pick a line that fits. If it hears nothing, nothing pops up.

Is my video or audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Sound and video are processed on your phone and never uploaded. There's no account and no server. The microphone and camera are only used while the dub screen is open. A clip leaves your phone only when you share it yourself.

Which pets does it work with?

It's made for dogs and cats. Other pets can be filmed, but the sound recognition and the lines are written for barks, meows and friends.

Which languages are there?

Subtitles come in 59 languages, translated on your phone. Each language is downloaded from Google once, then works offline. The full list: Afrikaans, Albanian, Arabic, Belarusian, Bengali, Bulgarian, Catalan, Chinese, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Esperanto, Estonian, Finnish, French, Galician, Georgian, German, Greek, Gujarati, Haitian Creole, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Icelandic, Indonesian, Irish, Italian, Japanese, Kannada, Korean, Latvian, Lithuanian, Macedonian, Malay, Maltese, Marathi, Norwegian, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Tagalog, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu, Vietnamese, Welsh.

Is it free?

Yes. Live dubbing is free, with ads. Pro removes ads and unlocks every persona, language and style. Pro is a subscription with a free trial for new subscribers, or a one-time lifetime unlock, and there are optional add-on packs. Prices are shown in Google Play.

Are there ads on the camera?

Never. Ads only appear in the free version, and never on the camera screen. Pro has no ads at all.

Can it tell if my pet is upset, sick or anxious?

No. Pawdub makes no claims about your pet's feelings, health or behaviour. A growl is just a growl sound to it. If you're worried about your pet, talk to a vet.

Who is it for?

Pet people aged 13 and up who like posting their pets. It is not made for children.

When can I get it?

It's on Google Play for Android now.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't post videos of your home or personal information.
